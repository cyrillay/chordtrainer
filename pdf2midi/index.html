PDF to MIDI, note for note.

Drop a score exported as PDF from MuseScore, Dorico, Sibelius or Finale and get a MIDI file back — pitches, rhythms, ties, tuplets, grace notes, key and time signatures.

Why so exact? Those PDFs are not pictures: every notehead, clef and accidental is a symbol from a music font placed at a precise position, and every stem, beam and barline is a vector line. We read that structure directly instead of guessing from pixels, so there is no blur, skew or noise to misread. Scanned or photographed scores are images and can't be read this way.
