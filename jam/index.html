Hall of fame
    House rules
    • Each chord is judged on its own: hold its notes in time with the band.
    • The bass has the root, so you only need the 3rd and the 5th (triads) or the 3rd and the 7th (seventh chords). Any octave, any voicing.
    • Land on the downbeat for a Perfect. Within the first beat is Good. Later is Late. Nothing, or three wrong notes, is a Miss.
    • 9ths, 11ths and 13ths are never wrong. They score a Spicy bonus.
    • Keep a streak and the band gets louder. Miss and they thin out.
